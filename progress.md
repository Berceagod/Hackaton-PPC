## Open design points
- Large bodies and RabbitMQ: queue messages for `/ingest` (≤10 MB) can carry a reference to a staged object; chunked/large uploads bypass the broker and stream directly to storage (decided in ADR-0010: chunks staged on S3, assembled by streaming).
- "Ack only after durable write": a persistent RabbitMQ message with publisher confirm counts as durable for `/ingest`; chunk path acks per chunk after durable chunk write.
- Dedup 409 semantics: ADR-0004.
